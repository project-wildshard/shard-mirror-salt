# Mirror Salt's look pass

> Written by: Claude, 2026-11-20

MIRROR-SALT-ZONE MSZ40, one honest pass per region (J281). Nine fixed camera domes (the look loop's nine cameras: the
Start Yard, the Arch Line, the Mirror, the Brine Pans, the Old Salt Works, the Wreck Field, the Glass Gorge, Colossus
Hollow and the Sun Lookout), shot before and after on the same frames; a blind Opus judge (J430) read the before board.

What changed, from the judge's list, form first:

- **The Mirror** was the same crust-speckled flat as everywhere else. The salt crust now keeps off the Deep Mirror, so the
  west is one unbroken sheet, and the crust is thinner zone-wide (the noise threshold 0.5 to 0.56). The sunken land-sail
  is 1.8 times its size, so it reads across the Mirror.
- **The Wreck Field** was metre-high debris in dark wood. The wrecks are sun-bleached timber now and their masts stand
  7.5 m (3 m when stripped), with the torn cloth cut to match.
- **The Glass Gorge** was knee-high shards. By the gorge the glass grows to walls of about 20 m, thinning down the road,
  with more clusters; the Colossus's third phase now stands among them.
- **The Arch Line** was smooth plastic hoops. The arches are hewn sandstone in nine blocky courses with block feet.
- **Everywhere:** the mesas' low faces are a deeper rust, the haze is thinner (0.0026 to 0.0019), and the flood is a cooler
  sheen, so the far regions keep their colour.

Left for later: the Old Salt Works is small from above; the ring berm's comb shading; the Hollow's floor is a flat disc.
