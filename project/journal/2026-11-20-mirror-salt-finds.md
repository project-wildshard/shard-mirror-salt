# The lost race pennants

> State: 2026-11-20. MSZ38 on a held branch.
> Written by: Codex

Ten pennants from the first Salt Cup cover all seven regions. The tenth
one earns Colours of the First Cup and unlocks the ivory sail at its
personal Start Yard dock. The flags and achievement come from the
normal quest and ledger paths.

The sunken sail keeps its iron-banded chest. Two more stand by the salt
works and the lookout. Each opens for its own player and pays once.
Four signs use the yard's board painter and tell how the old works and
the Cup shared a timekeeper. The existing sail, pennant and chest meshes
supply all the props.

The cooked-product tape takes all ten pennants, proves the reward is
locked at nine, mounts the ivory sail and refuses a second player.
It checks an attempted repeat, then opens each chest and reads each
lore sign.
