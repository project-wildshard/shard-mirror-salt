# Mirror Salt vehicle sounds

> Written by: Claude (mini-msz15)

MSZ15 names each kind's sounds. The buggy plays its motor loop, its
skid, a stone impact and the horn; each land-sail plays the luff of
its cloth as its loop and the creak of its frame as its skid, a wood
impact and the same horn. The recordings are MSZ39's five original
clips; each cue's voice is the recording's own path, which the engine
plays directly (engine 7504f23ce or later; older engines refuse the
cue as an unknown catalogue voice, so this waits for MSZ21's pin).
